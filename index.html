Madhuram-v1

Our latest model has 151 million parameters and was pretrained on one trillion tokens. The models we compare it with are larger, and were trained on six to nineteen times more data.

After post-training, it scores above Gemma 3 270M on every benchmark except GPQA Diamond, where all three models are at chance. It leads LFM2.5-230M on BBH, MMLU Pro and GSM8K with chain of thought, and trails it on instruction following, code and GSM8K without chain of thought.

Task-specific finetuning

At this size a model is cheap and quick to specialise. We finetune Madhuram on your labels for classification, extraction or routing, and hand back a model that fits your task rather than a general one that approximates it.
