Introduction

This Privacy Policy (“Privacy Policy” or “Policy”) applies to your access to and use of the website available at www.maruthlabs.com (“Website”), which is owned, operated, or controlled by JOBSPHERE TECH PRIVATE LIMITED, doing business as MaruthLabs (“MaruthLabs”, “Company”, “we”, “our” or “us”).

MaruthLabs is an artificial intelligence research and technology company headquartered in New Delhi, India. The Company develops language models and explores efficient AI architectures, including research relating to attention mechanisms, positional embeddings, computational efficiency, and the deployment of AI capabilities across cloud-based and edge-device environments.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with any additional privacy notice or terms that may apply to a specific service, product, application, or activity offered by MaruthLabs.

By accessing or using the Website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required under applicable law, such consent will be obtained through an appropriate mechanism. Accessing the Website alone will not be treated as consent to processing for which separate consent is legally required.

Material changes to this Privacy Policy will be notified at least five (5) days in advance through email, a prominent notice on the Website, and/or public announcements through MaruthLabs’ official social media accounts, as appropriate. Where required under applicable law, MaruthLabs will obtain fresh consent before implementing changes that require such consent.
