Madhuram-v1

A small model pretrained from scratch on one trillion tokens, then post-trained, and compared with models trained on two to nineteen times as much data.

Published
October 2026
Parameters
151 million
Pretraining
1 trillion tokens

Training data

Madhuram-v1 has 151 million parameters and was pretrained on one trillion tokens. We compare it with SmolLM2-135M, Gemma 3 270M and LFM2.5-230M, which were trained on two to nineteen times as much data.

Pretraining

As a base model, before any post-training, Madhuram-v1 has the best score on 9 of the 17 benchmarks, including both coding tasks, and the highest commonsense average. LFM2.5, trained on nineteen times as much data, leads on MMLU, CommonSenseQA, GPQA Diamond, TriviaQA, ARC Challenge and MATH-500.

All benchmarks are 0-shot except MBPP, which is 3-shot, and MATH-500, which is 4-shot and scored with math-verify. Every model ran on the same harness.

Post-training

We post-trained Madhuram-v1 and evaluated it on the same harness as Gemma 3 270M and LFM2.5-230M. It scores above Gemma 3 on every benchmark except GPQA Diamond, where all three models are at chance. It leads LFM2.5 on BBH, MMLU Pro and GSM8K with chain of thought, and trails it on instruction following, code and GSM8K without chain of thought.

Pass@k

Pass@k samples k answers per problem and counts the problem as solved if any of them is correct. It shows how much a model can solve when it gets more than one attempt.
